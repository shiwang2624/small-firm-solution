Privacy Policy

SmallFirm Solution · smallfirmsolutions.in · effective 24 October 2026

Short version: your books stay on your device. We do not have a server that stores your invoices, customers or items, and we do not sell data.

1. What the app stores, and where

DataWhere it livesWho can see it
Firms, parties, items, invoices, payments, expensesYour device — browser storage in the web app, app data folder in the desktop appYou
Business profile (name, GSTIN, address)Same as aboveYou
Backups you exportA file you choose to save or shareYou and whoever you send it to

2. What we do not collect

  • No account is required; we do not hold your books in the cloud.
  • No advertising or third-party analytics trackers are bundled in the app.
  • No access to your camera except while you are actively scanning a barcode; frames are processed on-device and not uploaded.

3. Camera permission

If you use barcode scanning, the browser or operating system asks for camera permission. Video is decoded locally for the barcode; nothing is recorded or transmitted. You can revoke permission at any time in your system settings.

4. Payments

If you buy a paid plan, payment is handled by a third-party payment gateway. We receive only the confirmation and basic billing details (name, email, amount) needed to issue a receipt — never your full card number.

5. Your responsibilities

Because the data is stored by you, you are the controller of any personal data you enter about your customers. Keep backups, secure your device, and delete data you no longer need.

6. Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it — delete a firm inside the app, clear the browser's site data, or remove the desktop app's data folder. Exported backups are under your control.

7. Children

The Software is a business tool and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
